Methodology & sources

Equation: Predicted lake AF = acres × modeled reduction in agricultural depletion (AF/acre) × assumed delivery share. Score = predicted lake AF ÷ total public cost × 1,000. Total public cost = acres × published/entered payment per acre × (1 + overhead rate).

2026 Utah study: Opportunities and Costs for Agricultural Water Optimization. Its coefficients are modeled alfalfa depletion reductions, not causal regression betas. Official rates and watershed groups: Great Salt Lake Preservation Program. Confirm eligibility, rights and quoted split-season payment with the state.

Limitations: Only applicable to alfalfa-style modeled scenarios. No historical farm-consumption adjustment, downstream routing model, additionality verification, applicant participation curve, or statutory water-right prioritization is currently implemented. The watershed name changes the published price only. It is not proof that water from that farm will reach the lake. A real $80M allocation requires eligible applicant records and a constrained optimizer, not only rank sorting.
